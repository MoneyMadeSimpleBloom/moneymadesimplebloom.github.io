Start a Budget When Money Is Tight

List essential bills first, set aside money for food and transportation, then assign the remaining amount to savings or other goals.

The 3-Bucket Method

Separate your plan into essentials, flexible spending, and future goals. The percentages can change depending on your income and bills.

Small Savings Add Up

Choose one repeat expense to reduce, automate a small transfer when possible, and review your progress once a month.
